a collaborative sense-making

Singapore’s urban landscape is built on modular repetition. The HDB comes to mind: fixed forms, in which life is constantly rearranged through routines, relationships and cultural expression.

This artwork applies a similar logic to cultural motifs, translating them into generative pattern units. Developed using a custom modular web-based tool created by the artist, this composition reflects how shared structures can shape a textured community.

SINGAPORE: LIMITLESS BY DESIGN

Over the past year, Dsg has been developing its fourth Design Masterplan to guide the growth of Singapore’s design industry over the next decade, from 2026 to 2035.

Titled Singapore: Limitless by Design, the publication was planned for release in the first half of 2026, with each chapter opening with a commissioned piece that reflects this spirit. This small series of artworks also extends into Dsg’s social media campaign.

The invitation asked for a response to the prompt limitless possibilities by design.

DESIGN 2035 MASTERPLAN→


MODULAR REPETITION

The work draws from a city made through repeatable forms: blocks, corridors, void decks, windows, routines and the countless small adaptations that happen inside them.

Rather than treating repetition as uniformity, the composition uses it as a structure for variation, gathering cultural motifs into a shared visual field.


CUSTOM TOOL

A modular web-based tool was created to compose the artwork. Motifs become tiles, tiles become pattern units, and pattern units can be arranged into larger structures.

The tool makes the artwork behave like a system: fixed enough to hold a shared logic, flexible enough to keep changing through placement, scale and combination.




TEXTURED COMMUNITY

The final composition sits between planning and improvisation. It borrows the language of a roadmap, but lets pattern, density and overlap suggest a more lived form of mapping.

Shared structures become a starting point for difference: a way to see community as something arranged, rearranged and collectively made.
